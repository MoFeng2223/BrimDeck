New
- New battery levels of wireless peripherals.
- New clipboard and file transfer.
- Custom fonts are supported (Settings > General > Appearance and language > Interface font).
- New "Background transparency" setting (Settings > General > Appearance and language).
- New "Resource use" setting with three levels: Saver, Balanced and Smooth (Settings > General). It replaces the "Free memory when idle" switch.
- New "Keep above other windows" switch (Settings > General > Window behavior), on by default. When it is off, the collapsed view can be covered by other windows, and the panel comes to the front when it expands.
- New "Allow window capture" switch (Settings > General > Compatibility), off by default. When it is on, recording software such as OBS can capture the panel on its own with window capture, but BrimDeck appears in the Alt + Tab list.
- New "Mouse pass-through" mode (Settings > General > Compatibility), suited to playing games.
- New "Expand on" setting (Settings > General > Hover and animation): choose "Hover" or "Click".
- New "Shortcuts" page in Settings: shortcuts can expand a given page, switch between the notch, the capsule and the indicator, and more.
- New "Quick actions" page under Music in Settings: drag with the mouse or swipe with two fingers on a touchpad on the Music page to change tracks, switch players or change the volume.
- New "Ignored apps" under Music (Settings > Players): what an ignored app plays is no longer shown.
- GPU usage in System monitor can be read from the graphics driver or from Windows (Settings > Display and readings > Sources).
- "Double-click a resize handle" has a new "Hide handles" option (Settings > Collapsed view > Shortcuts).
- Cursor supports the new plans merged with Grok; older plans are shown as before.

Improvements
- The quota ring no longer shows the highest of several quota limits. Apps with both a 5-hour and a weekly quota show two concentric rings.
- The expanded panel now snaps to the top of the screen only when the capsule's "Vertical offset" is 10 DIP or less (it was 20 DIP).
- When usage is synced by hand, the sync button keeps turning until reading finishes.
- Some text in Settings, some animations and some visual details are improved.

Fixes
- Fixed CPU readings in some cases briefly being too high or showing "—".
- Fixed the core columns when collapsed going past their space and the rightmost ones being cut off on CPUs with many cores.
- Fixed the core columns when collapsed occasionally missing a few cores.
- Fixed temperatures not being read on some NVMe drives, such as the Kingston KC3000.
- Fixed one side of the page being left being cut off when switching pages.
- Fixed blank space being left in the app rules in Settings after the list is collapsed or an app is removed.
- Fixed KuGou Music being reopened after it is closed once full control is on.

新功能
- 新增无线外设电量显示功能。
- 新增剪贴板与文件中转功能。
- 支持自定义字体设置（设置 > 常规 > 外观与语言 > 界面字体）。
- 新增“背景透明度”设置（设置 > 常规 > 外观与语言）。
- 新增“资源占用”设置，分为省资源、平衡、流畅三档（设置 > 常规），替代原来的“空闲时释放内存”开关。
- 新增“显示在其他窗口上方”开关（设置 > 常规 > 窗口行为），默认开启。关闭后，收起状态可以被其他窗口遮挡，展开时显示在最前面。
- 新增“允许录制软件捕获窗口”开关（设置 > 常规 > 兼容性），默认关闭。开启后，OBS 等录制软件可以用窗口采集单独捕获面板，但会出现在 Alt + Tab 窗口列表中。
- 新增“鼠标穿透模式”（设置 > 常规 > 兼容性），适合玩游戏时使用。
- 新增“展开方式”（设置 > 常规 > 悬停与动画），可选择“悬停展开”或“点击展开”。
- 设置新增“快捷键”页，可以通过快捷键展开特定页面，切换刘海、胶囊、指示条等。
- 音乐新增“快捷操作”设置页，在音乐页面上拖动鼠标或用触控板双指滑动，可以切换歌曲、切换播放器或调节音量。
- 音乐新增“忽略的应用”（设置 > 播放器），被忽略的应用正在播放的内容不再显示。
- 系统监控的显卡占用率可选择从显卡驱动或 Windows 读取（设置 > 显示与读数 > 来源）。
- “双击调整把手”新增“不显示把手”选项（设置 > 收起外观 > 快捷操作）。
- Cursor 支持与 Grok 合并后的新套餐，老套餐的显示方式不变。

改进
- 不再取多个配额限制的最大值作为配额环显示内容。同时有 5 小时额度和每周额度的应用，配额环显示为同心双环。
- 胶囊的“垂直偏移”不超过 10 DIP 时（原为 20 DIP），展开的面板贴紧屏幕顶端。
- 手动同步用量时，同步按钮一直转动到读取结束。
- 设置项部分显示内容优化，部分动画优化，部分显示效果优化。

修复
- 修复部分情况下，CPU 读数短暂偏高或显示“—”的问题。
- 修复 CPU 核心较多时，收起时的核心柱超出宽度、最右侧几根被截掉的问题。
- 修复收起时的核心柱偶尔少几根的问题。
- 修复金士顿 KC3000 等 NVMe 硬盘读不到温度的问题。
- 修复切换页面时，离开的页面一侧被截掉的问题。
- 修复设置中应用程序规则收起或删除应用后留下空白的问题。
- 修复开启酷狗音乐完整控制后，退出酷狗时酷狗被重新打开的问题。
